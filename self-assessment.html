Professional Introduction

🛠️ From Mechanical Logic to Digital Insights "I spent 40 years debugging systems the size of a city block. Now, I’m doing it in the IDE."

After four decades specializing in the maintenance and repair of complex papermaking machinery, I decided to transition my lifelong passion for problem-solving into a B.S. in Computer Science (Concentration in Data Analytics), graduating in Fall 2026.

Why Me?

Most developers learn logic through a screen. I learned it on the factory floor, where "debugging" meant keeping high-speed industrial systems running under pressure. My career transition is fueled by a desire to bridge the gap between heavy industry and modern computing.

The Industrial Perspective: I understand the "hardware" of the world. Whether it’s a 3D rendering pipeline in OpenGL or a predictive data model, I view code as a tool to optimize real-world performance.

My background in mechanical systems gives me a unique edge in Data Analytics—I don’t just see numbers; I see the physical processes they represent.

Transitioning to software engineering at 73 isn't just a career change; it’s a testament to my commitment to lifelong learning and my ability to master complex new architectures quickly.
